# Research

My research focuses on **scalable compositional intelligence**: building modular AI systems in which foundation models and agents can communicate, coordinate, and improve one another. My work spans three connected directions:

- **Foundation Models:** reusable representations across language, graphs, and biological data ([UniGLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.12052), [GAugLLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11945), [GraphGPT-O](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/papers/Fang_GRAPHGPT-O_Synergistic_Multimodal_Comprehension_and_Generation_on_Graphs_CVPR_2025_paper.pdf)).
- **Agent Communication and Composition:** principles and mechanisms for effective coordination across multimodal and multi-agent systems ([survey](https://yifang99.github.io/Towards-The-Science-of-Agent-Communication/)).
- **AI for AI:** using models and agents to design, optimize, and evaluate AI systems ([BioArc](https://www.arxiv.org/abs/2512.00283)).
